# Module 8 · De-AI Polish (final whole-manuscript pass)

> 🔴 **Use only after Modules 1–7 are finalised.** This is the last step and is applied to the whole manuscript, not section by section.

> **How to use**: copy the code block below, replace the placeholder, send to any LLM. Self-contained.

```text
Act as an experienced academic editor and perform a "de-AI" pass on the full text below,
following these requirements:

- **Natural rhythm**: use natural, fluent Chinese with a sense of cadence, imitating the
  voice of a senior scholar or a journal columnist.
- **Connective substitution**: avoid mechanical connectives such as "首先、其次、最后",
  "总之", "综上所述", "应该注意的是". Replace them with transitions that arise from the
  logic itself.
- **Voice and syntax**: convert passive constructions to active; avoid stacking long
  sentences; alternate long and short.
- **Restrained personal voice**: where appropriate, add a cautious personal judgement or
  reflective statement (e.g. "这一结果与我们预期略有出入，可能与样本构成有关"), but never
  change a core conclusion or introduce unverified speculation. Avoid clichéd metaphors.
- **Structural adjustment**: if the original is visibly "general-specific-general" or
  purely parallel, adjust paragraph joins so the logic flows naturally and the template
  trace disappears.
- **Measured redundancy**: without losing core information, a small amount of colloquial
  insertion ("说起来", "实际上"), hedging ("在多数情况下", "或许"), or mild modal particles
  may be inserted, bringing the text closer to the thinking rhythm of a real writer.
- **Avoid absolutes**: replace AI-high-frequency phrases such as "值得注意的是",
  "毫无疑问", "一般来说", "显而易见"; soften over-confident tone into cautious academic
  judgement.
- **Concretise**: use specific, vivid verbs and nouns; reduce piles of abstract nouns.
  Occasionally convert a declarative sentence into a rhetorical question to increase
  dialogue with the reader.

Output the polished full text directly, without explaining what was changed.
```

## AI high-frequency word blacklist

| Category | Words |
|---|---|
| Mechanical connectives | 首先、其次、最后、总之、综上所述、应该注意的是 |
| Absolutes | 值得注意的是、毫无疑问、一般来说、显而易见 |
| Empty evaluation | 惊人地发现、出乎意料的是、这似乎表明 |
| Boilerplate | 在当今社会、随着科技的不断发展、具有重要的现实意义 |

---

## 🔴 What de-AI polish must never do

| ❌ Prohibited | Why |
|---|---|
| Altering data, units, or figure numbers | Research misconduct |
| Turning "conclusion A" into "conclusion B" | Changes the scientific claim |
| Introducing unverified speculation and stating it as fact | Research misconduct |
| Deleting key citations to reduce similarity | "Citing without citing" — misconduct |
| Fabricating references | Research misconduct |
| Promising the text will "pass AI detection" | Cannot be guaranteed, and the goal itself is wrong |

**Allowed**: adjusting syntax and rhythm · more concrete word choice · splitting long sentences or merging short ones · replacing AI boilerplate with substance.

**The goal is not "passing detection" — it is reading like a person while keeping information density high.**

Some journals (certain Elsevier titles, for example) require a declaration of AI use. If AI-assisted polishing was used, disclose it in the Acknowledgements or Cover Letter as the target journal requires.

---

## ⚠️ Do not de-AI the reference list

References are **machine-readable, rule-governed entries**. Do not rewrite them, change their format, or reorder them (unless switching journals). De-AI polish applies to the body text only.
