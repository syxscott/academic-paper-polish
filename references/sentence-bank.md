## 一、引言：引出研究不足（用 However 引导）

```
Previous research (studies, records) has (have) investigated
Previous research (studies, records) has (have) studied
Previous research (studies, records) has (have) studied (with respect to) ...

  failed to consider
  ignored
  misinterpreted
  neglected to ...
  overestimated / underestimated
  ... is misleading

thus, these previous results are inconclusive / misleading / unsatisfactory / questionable / controversial
Uncertainties (discrepancies) still exist ...

However, little information ...
However, little attention has been paid to ...
However, little work has been done on ...
However, little data are available on ...
However, little research has focused on ...

or few studies ...
or few investigations ...
or few researchers ...
or few attempts ...
or none of these studies

has (have) been less focused on ...
attempted to ...
conducted ...
```

### 强调自己研究重要性的 5 种切入

| # | 切入 | 句式 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **时间问题** | 大量提及较老问题的研究及重要性 → However，对时间尺度较新的问题研究不足 |
| 2 | **物性/研究手段** | 提出当前比较流行的方法以及物质性质 → However，对你所研究方向和方法研究甚少 |
| 3 | **研究区域** | 总结相邻区域或其他区域的研究 → However，该区域研究不足 |
| 4 | **不确定性** | 虽然前人研究很多，但存在两种或多种观点 → these uncertainties / ambiguities 值得进一步澄清 |
| 5 | **提出假设验证** | `We aim to test the feasibility (reliability) of the ...`<br>`It is hoped that the question will be resolved (fall away) with our proposed method (approach).` |

## 二、引言：措辞正反例

| ❌ 避免 | ✅ 改用 |
|---|---|
| The deficiency of Wang's approach is … | However, the mechanism has not been fully understood. |
| The problem of these papers … | None of the other phases have been examined in detail. |
| for the first time | 直接陈述与前人工作的具体差异 |
| 首次提出 / 首次发现 / 达到国际先进水平 | 只陈述客观增量 |

**原则**：
- 不直接涉及作者和参考文献来说明问题
- 忌 *for the first time* —— 每篇论文都可以称为"第一次"
- "所谓第一次通常是指比较重要的研究进展"

## 三、摘要：分层指示词

| 层次 | 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 科学问题 | **However / yet …** | 然而… |
| 结果 | **We found that … / The results show that …** | 研究表明/结果显示… |
| 讨论 | **Based on …, we suggest that …** | 通过对…的分析，认为… |

## 四、讨论：常用动词

| 功能 | 动词 |
|---|---|
| 陈述论文的论点、作者观点 | suggest, report, present, expect, describe |
| 说明论证 | support, provide, indicate, identify, find, demonstrate, confirm, clarify |
| 推荐和建议 | suggest, suggestion, recommend, recommendation, propose, necessity, necessary, expect |

## 五、结论：局限性与未来方向（★可直接套用）

```
Despite its advantages, the [方法名] also has some limitations.
First, [限制1：受限于仪器/探针/方法本身，如只能在某电位区间测定].
Second, [限制2：导致不能做某类应用，如不适合野外测量].
Further research should focus on [识别合适的xxx] and [开发可支持xxx的装置].
```

**范例**（Fe/Mn 矿物氧化还原容量测定）：
1. First — 只能在 −0.4 V vs SHE 测 EAC、0.7 V vs SHE 测 EDC（受限于缺乏合适化学探针）
2. Second — 氧化条件下化学探针本身可变异性大 → 不适合**野外测量**
3. Further research should focus on — 识别合适化学探针 + 开发可支持可靠野外测量的装置

## 七、综述引言：三段式

```
[1 Subject background]
[一般性话题/关注领域，提供语境]

[2 Problem]
[趋势、新视角、缺口、冲突，或单一问题]
As ROS in the subsurface are a relatively new research frontier, the following
questions remain unanswered: (1)... (2)... (3)... (4)... (5)...

[3 Motivation / justification]
In this review, we first summarize recent progress ... In order to advance the
development of this new research frontier, we further provide i) ... ii) ...
```

**技术规格**：Tense 用**一般现在时**（描述自己的方法和结果用过去时）｜Citations **Many**｜Length 占正文核心部分 **10%–20%**

## 八、回复审稿人：四要素范式

```
→ Thanks for this concern. As suggested, we supplemented the experiments to monitor
  the variation of dissolved O2 concentration during N2 purging. Measurement showed
  that the concentration of dissolved O2 rapidly decreased to 0.27 mg/L within 10 min
  and then slowly decreased to around 0.1 mg/L (Fig. R1).

→ To address the reviewer's concern, the original statement "the solution was purged
  by N2 (99.999%) for at least 30 min to remove O2" was revised to "the solution was
  purged by N2 (99.999%) for at least 30 min to reduce the dissolved O2 concentration
  to below 0.1 mg/L (Fig. S5)." (Lines 193–195)

→ In addition, the Fig. R1 was copied as Fig. S5 in the revised supporting information.
```

**四要素**：① 感谢 → ② 补做实验给数据 → ③ 指出原文**哪一行（Lines）改成了什么** → ④ 附加材料编号对应

**六条纪律**：
1. 所有问题必须**逐条**回答
2. 尽量满足意见中需要补充的实验
3. 满足不了的也**不要回避**，说明不能做的合理理由
4. 审稿人推荐的文献**一定要引用**，并讨论透彻
5. 从审稿人角度理解并回答；拒绝修改意见要有理有据，**尽量不要与审稿人硬扛**
6. 感谢编辑和审稿人

## 九、编辑拒稿话术（识别用，不必回复）

```
(1) "We regret to say that ..."
(2) "We suggest that you might submit it to another journal"
(3) "An increasing volume of submissions prevents us from publishing all worthwhile
     manuscripts we receive. Competition for space in our journal has increased
     sharply in recent months."
```

> 编辑一开始说得再好也没用 —— 他们已选定刊物，主要因选择了他们刊物而要对作者表示感谢。
